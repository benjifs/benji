To me this felt like they thought of the last 30 mins of the movie and came up with some plot to fill the first two hours.

I think the last 30 mins were very impressive, specially [Courtney Grace](https://www.imdb.com/name/nm15031772/)'s performance. Its such a small part but it felt so real.

The last line in the film felt very underwhelming. While I get that themes of the movie this felt like an oversimplified way to get the message out, specially nowadays.
